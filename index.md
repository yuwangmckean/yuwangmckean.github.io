## 🔬 Professional Summary

Quantitative geneticist with 10+ years of experience in dairy cattle breeding across both academic research and commercial R&D. Proven ability to translate complex genomic theory into practical industry solutions, with expertise in genomic prediction, genetic diversity optimisation, and large-scale breeding scheme design. Currently focused on optimizing the use of whole-genome sequence data and developing genomic prediction models for New Zealand crossbred dairy cattle.
